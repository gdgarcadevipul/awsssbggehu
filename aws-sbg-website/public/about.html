Who We Are

Welcome to the AWS Student Builder Group at Graphic Era Hill University. Our goal is to explore cloud computing, AI, work on hands-on projects, and build a vibrant tech community aligned with the AWS ecosystem.

Our History

AWS Student Builder Groups (formerly AWS Cloud Clubs) are vibrant, student-led communities on post-secondary school campuses. Students come together to explore cloud technology, build innovative projects, and grow their technical skills. We operate under AWS community guidelines and use Meetup as our official platform for member interactions and event tracking.

Membership Eligibility

  • Open to all students of Graphic Era University.
  • No prior AWS experience required.

Member Benefits

  • 🚀 AWS workshops and hands-on learning sessions
  • 📚 Access to official AWS learning resources and programs
  • 💻 Cloud projects and certification guidance
  • 🎤 Tech talks, events, and collaborations
  • 🤝 Leadership and networking opportunities
  • 🎁 Eligibility for AWS swags, certificates, and cloud credits (subject to AWS policies)
