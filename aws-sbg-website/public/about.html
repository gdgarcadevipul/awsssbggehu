Who We Are

The AWS Student Builder Group at Graphic Era Hill University is an aspiring community of builders in Cloud, AI, Full Stack, and DevOps. We focus purely on active building — instead of dry theoretical courses, we run direct deployment workshops, engineering hackathons, and support open-source contributions.
